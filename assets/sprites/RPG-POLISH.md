## Prompt de José Luis
Use case: identity-preserve. Make a two-frame pixel art dialogue sprite sheet from the attached José Luis portrait. Transparent background. Exactly two equal square cells side by side in one horizontal 2:1 image, no border or labels. Left frame: his mouth closed, same neutral friendly determined expression as reference. Right frame: same face, head, shoulders, beard, striped gray shirt, lighting, size, position and pixel grid EXACTLY aligned, only mouth opens slightly for speaking, small visible dark mouth interior. Keep head and shoulders fully within each square frame. Same person, same recognizable face and chunky pixel-art style. No change to eyes, hair, body or pose between frames. No text, no other objects. This will alternate left/right cells for mouth animation.

Referencia: jose-luis.png.

## Prompt de Eric
Use case: compositing. Asset: one combined pixel-art game sprite, Dr. Eric seated astride ON TOP of an upright rocket while escaping, comical RPG villain. Reference 1 defines Eric identity: short light brown hair, black glasses, frowning eyebrows, white lab coat over light blue shirt and dark tie, dark trousers. Reference 2 defines rocket: upright silver body, red nose cone, cyan porthole, twin red boosters, orange exhaust. Show Eric sitting on the upper red nose cone like riding a horse, knees bent, a leg hanging on each side of the cone, hands holding onto the rocket top, lab coat tails fluttering. Eric must clearly be seated, NOT standing next to rocket, not inside window. Keep rocket vertical and entire rocket plus Eric and flames fully visible. Slight three-quarter view to make bent seated legs readable, but same front-facing visual style. Eric's seated body about one third of rocket height, recognizable face with angry/comical determined expression. Crisp chunky pixel art, limited palette, dark outlines. Truly transparent background, no ground, no pit, no text, no extra characters, no border. Single centered composed sprite ready to render as one object.

Referencias: dr-eric-standing.png, ignitia-rocket.png.
