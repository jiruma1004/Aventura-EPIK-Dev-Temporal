## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent pixel-art sprite sheet for a lighthearted educational RPG. Reference image is the identity/style reference for Iván: adult man, short dark hair, moustache and goatee, pale pink collared shirt. Create a NEW full-body sprite sheet, exactly four equal cells in one horizontal row, on genuinely transparent background. Each cell contains the SAME man at the same scale, with full head and shoes inside the cell and generous empty margins; crisp chunky 16-bit pixels, recognizable face, pink short-sleeve collared shirt, dark navy trousers, brown shoes. Cell 1: walking to screen right, left leg forward, looking around for someone. Cell 2: walking to screen right, right leg forward, searching expression. Cell 3: startled, arms spread, leaning back, right leg straight down, left knee bent, comedic surprised expression. Cell 4: same man upright with both arms reaching up beside his head, right leg straight down and left knee slightly bent, startled open mouth; this pose will be rotated upside down by the game to hang by the right ankle. Full body proportions consistent, no extra people, no text, no labels, no frame borders, no cables or rocket drawn (these are animated by the game). Four clear separate full-body frames, transparent alpha, no ground shadow.
